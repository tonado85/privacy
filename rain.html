Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Introduction

Rain Glass Simulator is designed to provide relaxing and entertaining virtual rain effects and glass animations. Your privacy is important to us.

Information Storage

Rain Glass Simulator processes simulation settings and related data directly on your device. The app does not upload your simulation settings or personal information to any server or the internet.

Personal Data Collection

Rain Glass Simulator does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address

Advertising

Rain Glass Simulator uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including device identifiers and advertising data, in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Rain simulation settings and related data are processed locally on your device and are not stored or transmitted to our servers.

Children's Privacy

Rain Glass Simulator is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
