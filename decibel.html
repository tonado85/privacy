Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Decibel Meter is a sound level measurement tool that uses your device's microphone to measure ambient noise levels in decibels (dB). Your privacy is important to us.

Microphone Permission

Decibel Meter requires microphone access to measure surrounding sound levels. The microphone is used only while you are actively using the measurement feature.

Audio Data

Audio input is processed locally on your device to calculate sound level measurements. The app does not record, save, upload, or transmit audio recordings to our servers or the internet.

Sound level readings and measurement history may be stored locally on your device, if this feature is available.

Information Storage

Decibel Meter processes and stores measurement settings, calibration values, and preferences directly on your device. The app does not upload your measurement data or personal information to our servers.

Personal Data Collection

Decibel Meter does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address

Advertising

Decibel Meter uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including device identifiers, advertising data, and usage information, in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

Decibel Meter may provide optional in-app purchases to unlock premium features. All payments are processed securely through Google Play.

We do not collect or store your payment information.

Data Security

Measurement settings, calibration values, and saved measurement records are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers. Audio input is processed locally and is not stored or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Decibel Meter is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
