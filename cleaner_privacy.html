Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Photo Cleaner is designed to help users find and manage duplicate, similar, and unnecessary photos and videos on their own devices. Your privacy is important to us.

Information Storage

Photo Cleaner processes photos and videos directly on your device to provide cleaning and organization features. Photo Cleaner does not upload your photos, videos, or personal files to any server or the internet.

Personal Data Collection

Photo Cleaner does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address

Photo Cleaner may request access to photos and videos on your device only for the purpose of scanning, identifying, and helping you manage your media files.

Advertising

Photo Cleaner uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including device identifiers and advertising data, in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Your photos, videos, and scan results remain on your device unless you choose to delete, share, or export them using your device's built-in features.

Children's Privacy

Photo Cleaner is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
