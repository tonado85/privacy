Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Lighter Simulator is designed to provide a virtual lighter experience with realistic flame animations and interactive visual effects. Your privacy is important to us.

Information Storage

Lighter Simulator processes lighter settings, animation preferences, and user customization options directly on your device. The app does not upload your settings or personal information to any server or the internet.

Personal Data Collection

Lighter Simulator does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address

Advertising

Lighter Simulator uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including device identifiers and advertising data, in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Device Features

Lighter Simulator may use device features such as touch input, screen interaction, and motion effects to provide an interactive virtual lighter experience.

These features are processed locally on your device and are not used to collect personal information.

Data Security

Lighter Simulator settings and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Children's Privacy

Lighter Simulator is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
