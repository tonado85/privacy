Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Ruler is a measurement tool designed to help users measure objects using their mobile device screen. Your privacy is important to us.

Information Storage

Ruler processes measurement settings, calibration preferences, and other app preferences directly on your device. The app does not upload your measurement data or personal information to our servers.

Personal Data Collection

Ruler does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address

Advertising

Ruler uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including device identifiers and advertising data, in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Device Features

Ruler uses screen interaction and touch input to provide measurement functions. The app may also use device display information to help calibrate the on-screen ruler for more accurate measurements.

Measurement interactions and calibration settings are processed locally on your device and are not used by the app to collect personal information.

Data Security

Ruler settings and calibration preferences are stored locally on your device, where applicable, and are not transmitted to our servers.

Children's Privacy

Ruler is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
