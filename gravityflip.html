Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Gravity Flip is designed to provide fun and challenging gravity-based arcade games. Your privacy is important to us.

Information Storage

Gravity Flip processes game settings and gameplay data directly on your device where possible. The app does not upload your personal information to our own servers.

Personal Data Collection

Gravity Flip does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address

Game Data and Leaderboards

Gravity Flip may store gameplay information such as scores, high scores, game statistics, player IDs, and leaderboard-related data to provide game features and improve your experience.

Some game data may be processed by third-party services used by the app, such as Google Firebase, depending on the features enabled in the app.

Advertising

Gravity Flip uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including device identifiers, advertising data, and usage information, in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics and App Services

Gravity Flip may use Google Firebase services to support app functionality, analyze app usage, and improve the user experience. Firebase may collect certain technical and usage information in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Gravity Flip takes reasonable measures to protect information processed by the app. Game settings and locally stored gameplay data are processed on your device where applicable. Information handled by third-party services is subject to the security and privacy practices of those services.

Children's Privacy

Gravity Flip is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
